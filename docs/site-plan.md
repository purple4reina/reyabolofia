# Site plan and research

Written 2026-10-21 for the redesign of reyabolofia.com from a student-resources
site into a performer's site.

## What other clarinetists' sites have

Sites reviewed: Anthony McGill (New York Philharmonic), Andreas Ottensamer
(Berlin Philharmonic), Martin Fröst, Julian Bliss, Kinan Azmeh.

| Content | How common |
| --- | --- |
| Large hero photo with name and instrument | All |
| Short bio on the home page, full bio page | All |
| Concert calendar with venues and ticket links | Most |
| Recordings / discography | Most |
| Video | Most |
| Press quotes | About half |
| Downloadable press photos with credits | Some |
| Contact (usually through management) | All |
| Streaming and social links | All |
| News or newsletter | A few |

Even famous sites have gaps: placeholder "Lorem ipsum" on a principal's home
page, video titles with no links, a nearly empty home page, a discography page
literally named "blank". A complete, polished site stands out.

## Who visits, and what they need

1. **Presenters and venues** deciding whether to book: a program with a clear
   idea, proof of quality (audio and video), community engagement (talks,
   classes), clear technical needs, and easy contact.
2. **Radio producers**: clean recordings with full track details (titles,
   performers, instruments), program notes online, a pronunciation guide, and a
   high-resolution landscape photo with the credit in the file name. Some
   stations want 2–3 pieces totaling 10+ minutes recorded within the past year.
3. **Orchestras and contractors** (sub lists, auditions): positions,
   instruments and doubles, region, excerpt recordings, résumé, contact.
4. **Press**: bios in several lengths, hi-res photos, quotes.
5. **Audiences**: upcoming concerts.

## Goals, and how this site meets them

1. **Credible in five seconds.** The hero is a Norm Eder portrait with the
   current title directly under the name.
2. **Hear him immediately.** One-click playback from the home page, with a
   player that keeps playing across pages.
3. **Something no one else has.** *New York Counterpoint* told as an interactive
   ring of ten speakers plus the live player, lit in sync with the recording.
4. **Easy to book.** A program page that answers a presenter's questions:
   length, performers, room needs, audience, the encore, and a booking email
   with the subject filled in.
5. **Press and radio served without back-and-forth.** Bios in three lengths
   with copy buttons, credited hi-res photos, a timed track list with
   personnel and recording details, program notes, and WAVs on request.
6. **Active.** Upcoming concerts from one data file; past dates drop off.
7. **Custom, fast, accessible.** A design built around the black-backdrop
   portraits, layered scroll effects, responsive images, reduced-motion
   support.
8. **His story.** Portland roots, the clarinet from his mother, music history,
   25 years of teaching.
9. **Findable and shareable.** Search metadata, structured data, a link-preview
   image, clean addresses (reyabolofia.com/the-jewish-clarinet).
10. **Stays current.** Evergreen copy; dated facts live in data files.

## Pages

- Home: hero, introduction, listen, *New York Counterpoint*, a quote,
  The Jewish Clarinet, orchestral work, upcoming concerts, contact.
- About: full bio, positions, education, honors, teaching.
- Listen: all recordings with notes; orchestral excerpts; for radio.
- The Jewish Clarinet: the program for presenters, with program notes, video,
  and logistics.
- Orchestral: for contractors and audition committees, with excerpts and the
  résumé (also as a PDF).
- Press kit: fact sheet, bios, photos, radio track list, coverage.
- Students: the earlier warm-ups, études, solos, and tips, restyled.

Left out on purpose: a blog or news feed (goes stale), a newsletter (no list
yet), and social media embeds.
